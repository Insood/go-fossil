# Project Brief

## Premise

The player pilots a drone through a fossilized junkyard full of buried consumer technology and occasional true fossils. Valuable objects are partially embedded in terrain and must be carefully cut free before they can be collected.

## Player Actions

- Fly through the junkyard
- Survey the ground for interesting embedded objects
- Use a small cutting laser to remove terrain around a target
- Pick up freed objects
- Avoid enemies and environmental danger

## World Tone

The setting should feel like an archaeological dig site crossed with an e-waste graveyard:

- dead hardware
- compressed layers of discarded technology
- relics from different eras buried together
- living scavengers and automated defenses reclaiming the space

## Gameplay Pillars

### 1. Precision Salvage

The core interaction is not simple collection. The player identifies an object, cuts away surrounding material, and extracts it without unnecessary exposure to risk.

### 2. Readable Spatial Navigation

Although the game world is 3D, the camera presentation should make terrain shape, object position, and danger easy to read from an orthographic isometric-like view.

### 3. Light Threat Management

Threats should create movement and decision pressure while the player is salvaging:

- security drones pressure open-air movement
- junkyard rats threaten low-altitude or grounded interactions

### 4. Discovery

Finding unusual buried artifacts should be rewarding even before a full progression system exists.

## Early Scope

Keep the first playable loop extremely small:

- one small chunk pair
- one drone
- one collectible object type
- one cutting interaction
- one hazard, if time allows

## Things To Avoid Early

- procedural world generation beyond what is needed for one test map
- large inventory systems
- complex combat
- multiple biomes
- deep narrative systems

## Definition Of A Good Prototype

An early prototype is successful if a player can:

1. move the drone through a 3D orthographic scene
2. visually identify a buried object
3. cut terrain around it
4. collect it
5. understand at a glance what threatened the attempt
